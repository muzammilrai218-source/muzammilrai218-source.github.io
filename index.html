🎬 Muzamil Uploader

A private, personal tool used by its owner to upload and manage videos on his own YouTube channels and TikTok accounts.

What it does
  • Uploads videos (title, description, tags, thumbnail, privacy, schedule) to the owner's own YouTube channels
  • Shows the owner his own channel and video statistics (views, subscribers, YouTube Analytics)
  • Lets the owner delete his own videos
  • Sends videos chosen by the owner to his own TikTok inbox as drafts; he then finishes and posts them in the TikTok app
Who can use it

Only the owner. It runs on the owner's private home server and is not offered to the public.
