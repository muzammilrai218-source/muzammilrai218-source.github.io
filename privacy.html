Privacy Policy — Muzamil Uploader

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Muzamil Uploader is a private, personal application operated by Muzammil Rai for managing his own YouTube channels and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to the public.

Google user data we access

  • YouTube upload (youtube.upload) — to upload videos chosen by the owner to his own channels.
  • YouTube account (youtube, youtube.readonly) — to read the owner's channel and video details and to delete videos only when the owner explicitly asks.
  • YouTube Analytics (yt-analytics.readonly) — to show the owner his own channel statistics.

How the data is used

The data is used only to provide the features above to the owner. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not shared with any third party, and not used to train any AI or machine-learning model.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens and channel details are stored only on the owner's private home server, protected with file permissions and accessible only from the owner's home network. Video files remain on the owner's own storage.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while a channel is connected. Removing a channel in the app deletes its stored token and details. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services

Muzamil Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. By using YouTube features you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see the Google Privacy Policy.

TikTok user data we access

  • Basic profile (user.info.basic) — the account's open ID, display name and avatar, so the owner can see which TikTok account is connected.
  • Video upload (video.upload) — to send videos chosen by the owner to his own TikTok inbox as drafts. The owner finishes and posts them himself in the TikTok app.
  • Direct post (video.publish, only if enabled) — to post a video chosen by the owner with the privacy level he selects.

TikTok data is used only to provide these features to the owner. It is not sold, not shared with any third party, not used for advertising and not used to train any AI or machine-learning model. TikTok tokens are stored only on the owner's private home server, like the Google tokens above, and are deleted when the account is removed in the app. Access can also be revoked in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions. TikTok's own handling of data is described in the TikTok Privacy Policy.
